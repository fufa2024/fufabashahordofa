About

The story behind the datasets

Statistician by training, storyteller by habit — here's how I got from questionnaires to policy rooms, and why the Afaan Oromoo work matters just as much.

Biography

I'm Fufa Basha Hordofa, a statistician and policy analyst based in Ethiopia. My work lives at the intersection of quantitative research, humanitarian planning, and education — projects where a regression table has to eventually become a decision someone is willing to act on.

Over the past decade I've supported graduate researchers preparing thesis defenses, contributed statistical modelling to studies on informal settlements and land policy, and helped humanitarian teams translate Sphere Standards into shelter-block layouts for displaced families. In parallel, I write and design educational and Christian content in Afaan Oromoo — a language I believe deserves the same editorial care as any other.
