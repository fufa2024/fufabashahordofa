Research

Research projects

Ten representative studies spanning informal settlements, education, agriculture, humanitarian planning, and public policy — each with objectives, method, and a downloadable brief.

01

Urban Development and Social Equity

Examining how informal settlement expansion in peri-urban Shaggar City reshapes residents' access to water, schools, and health posts.

Objectives

  • Map settlement growth using multi-year satellite imagery
  • Link land-cover change to household-level service access
  • Recommend equity-sensitive zoning adjustments

Method

NDVI/LULC classification in Google Earth Engine, cross-referenced with a 420-household survey and binary logistic regression.

02

Teaching-Learning Process Evaluation

A classroom-level evaluation of instructional practice in primary schools, comparing observed teaching methods against curriculum targets.

Objectives

  • Assess alignment between lesson delivery and curriculum standards
  • Identify structural barriers to effective teaching
  • Propose low-cost, high-impact interventions

Method

Structured classroom observation checklists combined with teacher and student surveys, analyzed in SPSS.

SPSSObservationEducation

03

Conservation Agriculture

A field-level study on smallholder adoption of conservation agriculture practices and their effect on soil retention and yield stability.

Objectives

  • Measure adoption rates across sampled kebeles
  • Model determinants of adoption using household characteristics
  • Estimate yield and soil-retention outcomes

Method

Multi-stage cluster sampling with a structured household questionnaire; probit and OLS modelling in STATA.

STATAProbitAgriculture

04

Humanitarian Assessment

A rapid needs assessment supporting site planning for an internally displaced persons settlement, aligned with Sphere Standards.

Objectives

  • Assess shelter, WASH, and protection needs
  • Cross-check findings against Sphere minimum standards
  • Inform a redesigned shelter-block layout for 1,060 residents

Method

Household-level rapid needs assessment, key-informant interviews, and GIS-supported site mapping.

05

Monitoring and Evaluation

Design and rollout of an M&E framework for a multi-year education program, including indicator tracking and midline review.

Objectives

  • Build a results framework with SMART indicators
  • Establish baseline and midline data collection tools
  • Deliver a midline performance report to program donors

Method

Logical framework analysis, indicator tracking tables, and a mixed-methods midline evaluation.

06

Policy Evaluation

An assessment of land-policy implementation outcomes under Proclamation 721/2011 and Oromia Directive 07/2018.

Objectives

  • Evaluate implementation gaps between policy text and practice
  • Identify determinants of successful implementation
  • Recommend directive-level adjustments

Method

Document review, key-informant interviews, and binary logistic regression on implementation outcomes.

Impact Assessment

Before-after impact assessment of a livelihoods program, isolating program effects from broader contextual change.
